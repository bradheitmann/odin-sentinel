# Intent card — odin-sentinel
ratification: pending-owner

- Underlying job (not the symptom): let one operator run a visible team of coding agents across different harnesses and know, from checked evidence rather than from the agents' own claims, who holds what authority, whether each instruction actually arrived, and whether each piece of work was independently verified before it lands.
- Beneficiary: the operator running a governed multi-agent team (often new to terminals and agents), and the PM, DEV, QA and ODIN roles that need one shared, checkable contract.
- Situation: agents in different harnesses (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Pi, Droid and others) run in visible terminal panes. Messages get stranded in input bars, panes look busy when they are idle, workers mark their own work as done, and readiness is assumed because a tool is configured. ODIN Sentinel ships the role contract, the protocol resources and the validators over MCP; it does not run the agents.
- Outcome (observable): a delivery-proof record, boot receipt, delegation packet or readiness claim goes through an ODIN validator and gets a typed verdict. A real delivery validates; a refusal, a stranded input or an unverified readiness claim does not pass as a delivery or as readiness.
- Non-goals: ODIN does not implement or QA-accept work, does not launch or staff teams without PM authority, is not a model router (that is evaluation-backed and owned elsewhere), and is not a dependency of any consuming project. Consumers may export tooling to ODIN; ODIN decides what to take in.
- The 3 guarantees that matter (name them; each gets a row in GUARANTEES.yaml): G1 a delivery is proven, never assumed; G2 governed readiness is fail-closed; G3 the published surface carries no private paths, private markers or secrets.
- Largest uncertainty: whether the validators match how real teams send and log messages. The first outside evidence (a 20+ seat team's 2,451-line proof log) shows the proof schema matches field for field, but 1,485 of those lines are busy-pane refusals that ODIN currently labels "invalid" instead of typing them as non-deliveries.
- Premise check — symptom named by the requester: "agents say they delivered or finished and it isn't true" / job it actually serves: give the operator checked, typed evidence of delivery, readiness and independent acceptance, so trust comes from validation instead of from the agent's word.
- Evidence lines (what this card is grounded in): AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md role contract (EXEC PM, TEAM PM, DEV, QA, ODIN); the fail-closed GOVERNED_READY rule in AGENTS.md; the 0.8.0 release (governance separated from selectable MCP capabilities); the 2026-10-29 Captain succession handoff for this repo (instruction revision, delivery versus receipt versus verified effect, exact workspace/surface/seat binding); the 2026-10-31 team-tooling export and its validator run against odin.validate_cmux_delivery_proof.
